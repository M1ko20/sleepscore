Privacy Policy

This is an English translation. The Czech version (Zásady ochrany osobních údajů) is the original; if the two differ, the Czech version prevails.

Here you will find what personal data the Sleepscore app (the “app”) processes about you, why, for how long, and what rights you have. We try to keep it as clear as possible. Your use of the app is also governed by the Terms of Use.

In short: We only collect what the app needs to work. We use your sleep data only for your cards, scores, challenges and statistics. We do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and we have no analytics or tracking tools. You can download everything in the app and delete your whole account at any time.

1. Who processes your data

The controller of your personal data is Adam Mikoláš (“we”). For any matter relating to personal data, write to us at adam.mikolas64@gmail.com. We have not appointed a data protection officer, as the law does not require one in our case.

3. What we do not do with your data

  • We do not sell or rent it.
  • We do not use it for advertising, marketing or profiling for third parties. We use data from Apple Health, in line with Apple’s rules, only for the app’s features.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools, advertising identifiers or cookies (not on this website either).
  • We do not give medical advice and we do not make any decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. The score is calculated automatically using a fixed formula, but it serves only for fun and comparison.

4. Who can see your data

  • Your friends see your nights, cards, photos, tags, comments, reactions, challenges, badges and the statistics on your profile.
  • All other users see only your username, display name and profile photo, so that they can find you and send you a friend request.
  • Participants in a group challenge see the other participants’ scores within that challenge.
  • Processors who provide technical services for us and may use the data only on our instructions:
    • Supabase Inc. – database, sign-in and photo storage. Data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
    • Apple Inc. – Sign in with Apple, notification delivery, Apple Health (runs on your device).
    • GitHub Inc. – hosting of this website (no cookies).
  • Public authorities, only where required by law.

Some processors are US companies, so data may also be accessed from outside the EU. In such cases the transfer is protected by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or, where applicable, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Anything you share further (for example a card image you send elsewhere using the Share button) is beyond our control.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Account data and content for as long as you have an account. You can delete individual comments, photos and reactions at any time.
  • When you delete your account, your data is removed from the database and storage immediately. It disappears from any backups within 30 days at the latest.
  • Technical logs for no more than 90 days.
  • Results of group challenges remain available to the other players (e.g. they can see their win), but no longer linked to your account.
  • Longer only if required by law or necessary to resolve a specific dispute or abuse.

6. Your rights

  • Access and portability: in the app under Settings → Privacy and my data → Download my data, you get all your data in a single file (JSON).
  • Erasure: in the app under Settings → Privacy and my data → Delete account.
  • Withdrawal of consent: you can turn off access to Health in iOS (Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices). Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it. To remove nights already uploaded, delete your account or write to us.
  • Rectification, restriction of processing, objection to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Complaint to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7, Czech Republic, uoou.gov.cz).

You can exercise your other rights by email at the address above. We will reply within one month. To avoid disclosing data to someone else by mistake, we may ask you to verify that the account belongs to you.

7. Security

Communication is encrypted (HTTPS), access to data is controlled by rules in the database (everyone sees only what they are allowed to), and photos are kept in non-public storage. However, no system is one hundred percent secure. If a breach occurs that would pose a high risk to you, we will let you know.

8. Age

The app is intended for people over 18 years of age. If we find out that an account belongs to someone younger, we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when we add a new feature. The effective date is shown at the top. We will inform you of material changes in the app in advance. If we wanted to use your data for a new purpose that requires consent, we will ask you.
